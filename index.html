SHUTTLES V3

Nine-hole sliders, fully visible at every position
The faint centre band is the five-hole fixed-board alignment. The complete 9-hole shuttle remains visible as it moves two notches left or right.
How to play

On your turn, either move one piece or slide one shuttle one notch. Red controls the red shuttles; Blue controls the blue shuttles. Either player may move the yellow shuttle unless the opponent moved it on the immediately preceding turn.

A piece moves any number of open holes in one orthogonal direction. It cannot cross a barrier, another piece, or a void where adjacent shuttles do not line up. An adjacent opposing piece may be jumped if the hole immediately beyond it is open; the jump ends the move.

Once a piece leaves its own starting block it cannot return. No piece may enter the opposing starting block until all five of that player's pieces have left home. First player to get all five pieces into the opposing starting block wins.
